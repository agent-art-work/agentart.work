**Rules change the entry method.**

[Hacker News guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) prohibit generated or AI-edited comments and automated posting; write there yourself. [LessWrong guidelines](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/tKTcrnKn2YSdxkxKG/frontpage-posting-and-commenting-guidelines) favor clear reasons, uncertainty and relevant disagreement.\
[Hugging Face guidelines](https://discuss.huggingface.co/guidelines) ask for reasoned discussion and no spam.

**First choice by question:** use Hugging Face to examine Agent behavior; compare Reddit, Substack and LessWrong for contribution and credit; use LinkedIn to hear about skill and work. These are candidates for the next test, not a ranking of whole platforms.

The list stays open. Further discovery can include Mastodon art communities, YouTube essay comments, artist run groups and university reading groups. Add an exact community and link before judging it.
