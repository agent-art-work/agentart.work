# Route B: create original content

### Route B Create original content that invites discussion

Choose this route when you have a useful question, example, work or test to put before people. You can choose it even when related discussions already exist.

1. **Make one thing to discuss.** Choose a short argument, artwork with process notes, a comparison, a small test or a short video. Connect it to one manifesto question.

2. **Give readers enough to respond.** State your view, show a real example or label a proposed test, and ask one clear question. The post should make sense without opening the manifesto.

3. **Choose a place and a way to reach people.** Try a relevant community that allows original posts, your own social feed, or an article with comments. Check who will see it. An article on your own site may need a short introduction in a relevant community where sharing is allowed.

4. **Open a clear invitation.** Ask for a specific experience, a counterexample or criticism of one claim. Say the manifesto is yours if you link it. Use a question people can answer without agreeing with you.

5. **Host the exchange.** Answer the points people raise, ask useful follow ups, make room for disagreement, and bring what you learn into the next work or post.

A related live thread is not required. Check how the first readers will find your piece, whether comments are open, and how you will host replies. Use the [platform check](../../templates/platform-check.md) and [trial report](../../templates/trial-report.md).
