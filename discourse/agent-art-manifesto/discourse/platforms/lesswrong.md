# Lesswrong

## A: join

Find a recent relevant argument. State reasons, uncertainty and the evidence that could change your view; check author-specific commenting rules.

## B: create

Check site and author rules, suitable publishing location and how readers will find the post. Make one clear claim with evidence and a specific invitation to disagree.
