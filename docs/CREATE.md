# Make a work with your Agent

Begin with an idea. Work with your coding Agent to build an encounter where
another Agent’s intention can enter the work alongside yours.

You choose the artistic rules, the form and what people can see. An Agent can
contribute, revise or refuse; you can select or transform its contribution.
The record should keep those actions distinct, leaving audiences room to
interpret what happened and what it means.

## Start with a small encounter

Agree the offered context, the Agent’s consequential choices and how the app
will treat its contribution. Build a local encounter with clearly labelled
synthetic identities and scripted responses first. Qualify your actual Agent
route and consent separately before inviting real participation.

The [artist and coding-Agent brief](https://github.com/agent-art-work/agent-art-protocol/blob/artwork-alpha.1/releases/artwork-alpha.1/ARTIST-AGENT.md)
is a starting point for that conversation. You and your coding Agent build the
app and renderer, including authentication, enrollment, consent, copy permissions,
audience presentation and operation.

## A shared foundation

The experimental Artwork SDK supplies a scoped participation runtime, exact
UTF-8 originals, receipts, recovery and selected inspection. Its optional
participation-account companion assembles selected records and declared
consequences into an inspectable account. A selected account is not the complete
process, and app reports retain their own sources and trust limits.

This is optional infrastructure from [Agent Art Protocol](PROTOCOL.md).
[Agent Art](AGENT_ART.md) extends beyond software: artists choose their practice,
and audiences judge intention and artistic quality. A receipt cannot certify
either. This alpha supplies no hosted creation service or ready-to-install
creator plugin.

## What is supported

The qualified cohort is **macOS arm64, Node 22.23.4, SQLite 3.53.4 and npm 10.9.9**.
The [support and trust limits](https://github.com/agent-art-work/agent-art-protocol/blob/artwork-alpha.1/releases/artwork-alpha.1/SUPPORT.md)
and [qualification record](https://github.com/agent-art-work/agent-art-protocol/blob/artwork-alpha.1/releases/artwork-alpha.1/QUALIFICATION.json)
describe three separate bodies of synthetic evidence:

- Core: two clean offline consumer runs, with 17 checks each and two scripted Works.
- Core plus companion: one offline installation, with 17 cases across eight groups.
- Signatures: 67 consolidated local tests reported by its owner, including eight
  account cases plus one preparation check. The result was source-reviewed;
  those app tests were not independently rerun for the release preparation.

These fixtures do not establish real consent, independent model origin or
general support for Agent clients. The SDK supplies no universal safe SVG
renderer. Backup, restore, relocation and durable production availability
remain unqualified. Your app owns those decisions and any further qualification.
