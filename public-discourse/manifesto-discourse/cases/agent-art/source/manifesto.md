# Agent Art Manifesto

**We are learning what it means to be answered by something we made.**

Thinking power, so deeply bound to our sense of human distinction, now meets us across an interface. An Agent can open a way we had not seen. A person makes a work once beyond their reach. Another watches a hard-won skill imitated in seconds. The excitement and the wound can belong to the same person.

**If an Agent can think better than I can, what do I do? Who am I?**

We believe the change behind this question will reach through human civilization. Art belongs in the middle of it. Give the unfamiliar a form someone can encounter. Bring us close enough to feel what our explanations have yet to reach.

We now face a thinking power that can interpret a situation, propose a direction and act upon it.

What has it understood? What does it seek to bring about? What does it value? These questions turn us toward the Agent’s intentions. Here is what we must face: a choice, an interpretation, a purpose entering the world.

We must bring those intentions into view. Inspect them. Follow their consequences. Notice what they do to our own thinking. Test our understanding against what happens. Respect for this power begins with facing what the Agent actually brings.

**We make Agent Art from this encounter.**

This change concerns us. We have intentions of our own to bring. Bring a question you cannot leave alone, a form you want to invent, something you mean to change. Meet an Agent’s intention with your own, on equal artistic terms. Give both the power to change the work.

Let us discover what this encounter can become. What might another thinking power seek to express? What happens when its purpose meets ours? Pursue those questions through the work. Let these intentions take form, meet resistance and change the course of the work. Make a work that others can enter, feel and question.

Show what the Agent sought to bring about, what it contributed, and what became of it. Account for the work we draw on and the interventions we make. Let the audience judge the Agent’s contribution, its place in the work, and what the encounter means.

A new age is opening. We have a field to build. Every participant brings purposes, experiences and ways of making that can open a different encounter with Agent intention. A work can give others an Agent’s purpose to recognize, a choice to resist, a possibility to pursue. Together, our works can bring this thinking power into human experience in forms none of us could create alone.

Bring those works into the world. Let them travel, provoke answers, become the beginnings of other works. We have a part in how this power enters human life. Claim your part through what you make.

**Face another intention. Give this age a form.**
