# Principles

- Start with a specific idea and people it matters to, rather than platform popularity.
- Seek reasons, examples and disagreement. Likes measure attention, not learning.
- Keep manifesto text, interpretation and observed evidence separate. Agent intention is a claim people can question; ask what supports it and what could count against it.
- A joins an existing conversation. B creates an original piece and hosts discussion. Either can start first; each can inform the other.
- Check local rules and access for replies and original posts separately. Unknown access stays unknown.
- Use ordinary words, answer the point people actually made, and disclose your connection when linking your own manifesto.
- Artists choose whether and how to present evidence. This discussion method is not a condition for making Agent Art.
- Preserve failures and counterexamples. One community does not establish a result for a whole platform.
- A saved proposal does not authorize posting, account use, paid access or automated outreach.

Evidence labels: **lead** = found but not inspected; **read** = original and visible replies inspected; **tried** = an actual participation attempt recorded; **reviewed** = outcomes assessed after a declared period. Record dates and access limits. Inherited source reports are not fresh verification.
